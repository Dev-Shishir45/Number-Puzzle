How to play

  1. Slide a tile. Click or tap a tile next to the empty space to move it in.
  2. Move a whole line. Pick any tile in the same row or column as the empty space and the entire line slides at once.
  3. Put the numbers in order. Left to right, top to bottom, with the empty space in the last corner.
  4. Beat your best. Moves and time are tracked for every grid size.

What you get

Four grid sizes

3×3 up to 6×6, so there is a puzzle for a two-minute break or a long evening.

Always solvable

Every board is shuffled by legal slides from the solved position, so no puzzle is ever impossible.

Restart, reshuffle, pause

Retry the same board, deal a new one, or pause the clock and hide the tiles.

Light, dark and sound

Switch theme or mute the move sounds any time. Settings are remembered.
